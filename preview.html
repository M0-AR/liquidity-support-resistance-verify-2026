Liquidity at Support & Resistance:
From Chart Line to Executable Quantity

A drawn line holds nothing. Only resting orders hold flow — and they get eaten, cancelled, and refilled every few seconds. This page proves it on live order-book data, then quizzes you from zero to pro.

The one sentence: A support/resistance line is a watchlist, not a wall — only currently-resting, still-present, large-enough limit quantity can oppose urgent flow, and none of those adjectives is guaranteed.

Start here — the only mental model (60 s)

Urgent orders execute now against whatever rests there. Resting (limit) orders wait in the book. A trade printing higher/lower than the last trade is price moving. "Patient vs urgent" describes this execution, not the person — the same trader can be either.

Then: your line adds zero shares (Step 1) · the 300-share wall gets eaten or cancelled (Step 2) · 5 attention reasons, each with a catch (Step 3) · liquidity spread + mutating + venue-partial (Step 4) · resting-now vs stop-later (Step 5) · bounce needs higher prints (Step 6) · breaks, sweeps, reclaim (Step 7–8) · old-order stories need book proof (Step 9).
